1. Executive Summary & Core Value Proposition

Conventional urban navigation systems (such as standard GPS devices and commercial routing engines) optimize routes strictly for minimal distance or shortest travel time. In major metropolitan areas like Bengaluru, this often funnels pedestrians, two-wheelers, and late-night commuters through dimly lit alleys, isolated ring road bypasses, or areas with sparse emergency service coverage.

WSZM bridges this safety gap by evaluating each candidate corridor across an empirical 5-Factor Safety Index. The system synthesizes official National Crime Records Bureau (NCRB) crime statistics, statutory Bengaluru District Court (eCourts) jurisdiction allocations, Bengaluru City Police coordinates, spatial street lighting coverage, commercial crowd presence, transit connectivity, and dynamic time-of-day risk fluctuations to grade road options into clear Green (Full Safety), Yellow (Medium Safe), and Red (Not Safe) tiers.

3. Mathematical Formulations & Algorithmic Models

3.1. The 5-Factor Weighted Safety Score

The safety score $S \in [0, 100]$ along any road corridor is formulated as a linear combination of five normalized safety factor functions $f_k \in [0, 1]$ weighted by time-of-day coefficients $w_k$:

S = 100 × [ w_crime · (1 - c) + w_police · (1 - p_dist) + w_lighting · l + w_crowd · f_crowd + w_isolation · (1 - i) ]

1. Low Crime Safety (1 - c)

Derived from locality incident baselines and NCRB metropolitan records. High incident density increases crime index $c$, lowering safety points.

2. Police Station Proximity (1 - p_dist)

Measures corridor closeness to the nearest Bengaluru police station: $p_{\text{dist}} = \min(1.0, d_{\text{min\_police}} / 5.0\text{ km})$. Routes within 500m score >90%.

3. Street Lighting Coverage (l)

Quantifies street lamp density and illumination across sampled road coordinates. Critical for preventing unlit blind spots.

4. Crowd & Open Shops (f_crowd)

Gauges human footfall, active commercial retail establishments, and transit nodes (Metro/BMTC stops) providing natural surveillance.

5. Area Connectivity / Low Isolation (1 - i)

Penalizes remote, desolate, or walled stretches with low street egress. Arterial ring roads and populated avenues maintain near-zero isolation ($i \to 0$).

3.4. Judicial Jurisdiction Intelligence (bengaluru.dcourts.gov.in)

To move beyond heuristic spatial assumptions, WSZM integrates official gazetted territorial schedules from the Bengaluru District & Sessions Courts portal (https://bengaluru.dcourts.gov.in/police-station-wise-magistrate/). Under Chief Metropolitan Magistrate (CMM) Notification ADM-I/04/2024 (Annexure-A), all 103 police stations across Bengaluru's 8 administrative divisions (Central, East, West, North, South, South-East, Whitefield, North-East) are mapped to 20 designated Magistrate Court divisions:

  • Statutory Coverage: 103 police stations, each linked to designated Magistrate Courts (e.g. Cubbon Park PS → XLI Addl CMM, Halasuru PS → X Addl CMM, Koramangala PS → XXXIX Addl CMM, Peenya PS → XXXI Addl CMM).
  • Court Complexes: Distinguishes territorial hearings between the City Civil Court Complex (Nrupathunga Rd) and Mayo Hall Court Complex.
  • Safety Impact: When calculating police proximity factor \(f_{\text{police}}\), the platform accurately links each corridor segment not only to Euclidean station distance but to the statutory law-enforcement and magistrate authority having legal jurisdiction over that segment.

4. Spatial Geometry & Navigation Algorithms

4.1. Great-Circle Distance (Haversine Formula)

Used for point-to-point corridor sampling, nearest police station matching, and live off-route drift tracking:

a = sin²(Δφ/2) + cos(φ₁) · cos(φ₂) · sin²(Δλ/2) c = 2 · atan2( √a, √(1−a) ) d = R · c (where R = 6,371 km)

4.2. Multi-Route Exploration & Selection State Machine

To give travelers complete clarity without visual overload, WSZM implements a 3-state route selection lifecycle:

1
Initial State: All Routes Shown (activeDirRouteIdx = -1)
When the routing engine computes alternatives, all candidate corridors are rendered at full standard visibility (5.5px stroke, 8px halo, 0.94 opacity, 1.0 badge opacity). No route is pre-dimmed, allowing the user to view all paths side-by-side.
2
Single-Click: Route Highlighting & Background Dimming (activeDirRouteIdx = idx)
Clicking any route line, marker badge, or sidebar card elevates the selected corridor (7.5px line, 12px white halo, 1.0 opacity, z-index 60, badge z-index 100) and dims all alternative corridors (3.5px line, 0.28 opacity, faint halo, badge opacity 0.40). The Route Studio highlights the matching card and factor gauge.
3
Double-Click: Single Route Locked & Alternatives Cancelled
Double-clicking locks that specific corridor to reach the destination and cancels all other alternatives (dirRoutes = [chosenRoute]). Alternative polyline overlays and badges are removed from the map. A "Show All / Restore Alternatives" button enables one-click restoration.

4.3. Live GPS Destination Tracker & Off-Route Detection

Continuous GPS Tracking

Utilizes navigator.geolocation.watchPosition with high accuracy mode, plotting a live blue marker, dynamic accuracy radius circle, and polyline breadcrumb trail.

Off-Route Detection (> 0.35 km)

Calculates minimum perpendicular distance from live coordinate $(lat_u, lng_u)$ to route segments. If drift exceeds 350 meters, the HUD triggers an orange off-route warning and offers one-click recalculation.

Arrival Detection (≤ 0.06 km)

When remaining distance to destination falls within 60 meters, the HUD status turns green with "Arrived at Destination safely".

Floating Navigation HUD

Displays real-time remaining distance (km), dynamic ETA based on speed, progress percentage bar, current corridor safety score %, and closest police station name with distance.

5. Security, Cryptography & Data Synchronization

5.1. Authentication Architecture

WSZM features a dedicated desktop login page enforcing Google Email ID identification with secure local password hashing:

  • Domain Enforcement: Rejects non-Google webmail domains (Yahoo, Outlook, Proton, iCloud, Zoho). Permits @gmail.com, @googlemail.com, and corporate Google Workspace domains.
  • Password Storage: Passwords are never stored in plaintext. They are hashed using PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256 with 120,000 iterations and a 16-byte random salt generated by secrets.token_bytes(16).
  • Timing-Attack Defense: Password hashes are validated using constant-time string comparison (hmac.compare_digest).
  • Session Management: Active session cached in data/user_session.json when "Keep me signed in" is checked; multiple accounts saved in data/auth_accounts.json for instant switching.

Because the Google Maps client runs inside an embedded iframe within the desktop UI, a robust message passing protocol keeps both interfaces synchronized:
